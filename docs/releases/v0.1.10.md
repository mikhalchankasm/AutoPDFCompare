# PDFCompare Local v0.1.10

Reworked change-significance metrics so that the primary percentage reflects drawn content, not the empty sheet — and classification uses a composite of three independent signals.

## Changed

- **Primary metric: % of drawn content (FG%).** Previously the headline was "% of sheet" (`diff_percent`), which on large A0/A1 engineering drawings (3–10% fill) made even significant changes read as "0.1%". FG% measures changed pixels relative to the drawn foreground, so a rebuilt node or new callout now shows a meaningful percentage.
- **Composite classification.** The level (minor / moderate / major) is the maximum across three independent signals, so a significant change is never masked by a sparse sheet:
  - FG%: ≥1% minor, ≥8% moderate, ≥20% major (ignored on sparse pages).
  - Largest change region: ≥100 mm² minor, ≥2500 mm² moderate, ≥10000 mm² major.
  - Zone count: ≥1 minor, ≥15 moderate, ≥40 major.
- **HTML report:** the change matrix leads with the FG% meter bar (heat colors at 1/8/20%); sheet diff% is a secondary plain number. The per-page toolbar, slider header, and sheet navigation all show FG% first. The page note reads "Changed ≈ X% of drawn content (Y mm², zones: N, max zone: Z mm²)".
- **Markdown and live reports:** FG% column added ahead of sheet diff%; the engineer report sorts by FG%.
- **GUI re-render tab:** "Drawn %" column promoted ahead of "Diff %".

## Added

- `foreground_sparse` flag: pages with less than 0.05% drawn content are flagged; FG% is unreliable on such pages (two lines on a blank A0 = 50%+), so classification falls back to absolute metrics (mm², zones). The page note omits FG% in this case.
- FG% is clamped to 100.0 (mask morphing could push it past 100 on near-empty pages).
- Legend updated to the 1/8/20% FG thresholds.

## Compatibility

- Legacy runs without `foreground_sparse` or `diff_foreground_percent` fall back to the old diff-percent classification and render "—" for missing fields — no errors.
